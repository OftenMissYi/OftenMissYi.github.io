Bionic Lower-Limb Exoskeleton

Design and performance analysis of an integrated knee joint drive unit for a bionic lower-limb exoskeleton.

Overview

Integrated Joint Drive Unit

This project focused on the mechanical and drive-system design of an integrated knee joint for a bionic lower-limb exoskeleton.

The work included actuator selection, gearbox design, mechanical integration and structural and performance analysis of the joint drive system.

Objective

Design Goals

  • Develop an integrated knee joint drive unit.
  • Select a suitable motor and transmission system.
  • Evaluate structural performance of the mechanical design.
  • Analyse the drive system under relevant operating conditions.
  • Balance torque, size, mass and mechanical performance.
System Design

Mechanical Architecture

The joint was designed as an integrated actuator module, combining the motor, transmission mechanism and structural components into a compact mechanical assembly.

Actuation Electric motor drive
Transmission Gear reduction system
Application Bionic lower-limb exoskeleton
Analysis Structural & performance analysis
Engineering Work

Design & Analysis

Motor Selection

Motor selection was carried out according to the required torque, speed, power and integration constraints of the knee joint.

Gearbox Design

The transmission system was designed to provide the required output torque while maintaining a compact and practical actuator architecture.

Structural Analysis

Structural analysis was performed to evaluate the mechanical behaviour of the joint components and identify potential design constraints.

Performance Analysis

The drive system was evaluated in terms of its mechanical and actuation characteristics to assess whether the design met the intended requirements.
