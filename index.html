About me

I studied Robotics Engineering at Chongqing University and joined TUM’s Robotics, Cognition, Intelligence master’s programme in October 2026.

During my degree, I worked on both the software and mechanical sides of robotics. I led the Minimum-Snap implementation in our UAV project and the wavelet-packet feature work in our EEG project. Other work involved writing embedded control software and designing an exoskeleton joint.

My internships gave me experience with industrial robots, PLCs and automated production systems. At TUM, I’m focusing on robot learning, motion planning and computer vision.
